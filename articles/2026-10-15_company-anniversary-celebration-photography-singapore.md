# Company Anniversary Celebration Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** A company's **10th, 25th, 50th or 100th anniversary** happens once. Plan the photography as a **year-long project**, not only one event:

- **Before**: portraits of founders, leaders and long-serving staff, the workplace today, and archive photos collected for a book or video
- **The celebration**: the guest of honour, speeches, the anniversary moment (cake, unveiling or toast), long-service awards and a **historic group photo**
- **After**: content for the anniversary year, such as a commemorative book, video, website timeline and social media series

Start **three to six months before** the celebration, so there is time for portraits, archive work and design.

## Founders, leaders and long-serving staff

These are the people the anniversary is about.

- **Portraits of founders and past leaders**, while they are available. For many companies, this is the most valuable part of the project.
- **Long-serving staff**: individual portraits and a group photo of everyone with 10, 20 or 30 years' service.
- **Short interviews** on video: how the company started, the hardest moments, what has changed.
- **The current leadership team**, formally and at work.

## Heritage and "then and now"

- **Collect archive photos** early: old offices, the first products, early teams, past events. Ask staff and retirees.
- **Scan prints and slides** at high resolution, and record who and what is in them while people still remember.
- **"Then and now" pairs**: photograph the same place, product or team today, from the same angle as an old photo.
- **Objects**: first products, uniforms, documents and awards, photographed carefully for the book.

## The celebration itself

Anniversary celebrations are often a dinner, a gala or a company-wide event. Cover:

- **Guest of honour and VIPs**: arrival, speeches and the main table
- **The anniversary moment**: cake cutting, unveiling a logo or plaque, launching the book or video, a toast
- **Long-service awards**: every recipient on stage, named. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/award-ceremony-photography-singapore "award ceremony photography"].
- **Performances** and the anniversary video being shown
- **The historic group photo**: everyone present, from a raised position. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/large-group-photo-corporate-event-singapore "large group photos"].
- **Guests, clients and partners** with the leadership team

## A commemorative book or video

- **Agree the outputs early**: a coffee-table book, a digital book, a video or all three.
- **Plan the photos with the designer**: book layouts need specific shapes, space for text and very high resolution.
- **Retouch carefully**: book portraits and archive scans need more care than event photos.
- **Video**: combine interviews, archive photos, the workplace today and the celebration. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-video-production-singapore "corporate video production"].

## Consent and archives

- **Ask permission** before publishing archive photos of former staff or clients, where possible.
- **Get consent** from staff featured in portraits and interviews.
- **Store the final archive** properly, with names and dates, so the next anniversary is easier.

## FAQ

### When should we start planning photography for a company anniversary?
Three to six months before the celebration. That leaves time to collect archive photos, photograph founders, leaders and long-serving staff, film interviews, and design a book or video.

### What should be photographed for a company anniversary?
Founders, leaders and long-serving staff, the workplace and operations today, "then and now" pairs with archive photos, and at the celebration the guest of honour, speeches, the anniversary moment, long-service awards and a historic group photo.

### How do we use old photos in an anniversary book or video?
Collect them early from the company, staff and retirees, scan prints at high resolution, record who and what is in them, and pair them with new photos taken from the same angle.

### What is a "then and now" photo?
A new photo of the same place, product or team taken from the same angle as an old one, shown side by side to show how the company has changed.

### How can anniversary photos be used after the celebration?
In a commemorative book and video, a monthly social media series, a website timeline, recruitment materials featuring long-serving staff, and thank-you messages to clients and partners.
