# Law Firm and Professional Services Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Law, accounting, consulting and other professional services firms sell **people and trust**, so their photography needs to be **consistent, professional and discreet**. The main needs are:

- **portraits** of partners and fee-earners that look the same across the whole firm
- **client seminars and briefings**, photographed without exposing confidential material
- **firm milestones**: partner promotions, new office openings, anniversaries and award nights
- **office and team photos** for the website, proposals and recruitment

Record a **portrait setup** that new joiners can match, keep **client names, documents and screens** out of frame, and follow the **firm's own publicity rules** before publishing.

## Portraits: one look for the whole firm

Partner and lawyer profiles are often the most visited pages on a firm's website.

- **Agree a standard**: background, lighting, crop, pose and retouching level.
- **Record the setup**, so new partners and associates photographed months later match.
- **Two versions**: a formal profile portrait and a more relaxed environmental portrait in the office.
- **Dress code**: business formal, consistent across the firm.
- **Schedule efficiently**: short slots in a meeting room, so fee-earners lose little time.

## Client seminars and briefings

Professional services firms run seminars on regulation, tax, markets and legal updates.

- **Speakers and panels**: well lit, facing the audience
- **The audience engaged**: questions and discussion, photographed so no single client is the focus without consent
- **Networking**: partners with guests, where guests agree
- **No confidential slides**: agree which sessions may show slides in photos

## Confidentiality

Client confidentiality is central to professional services:

- **No client names** on whiteboards, screens, files or meeting room displays in photos.
- **No documents**: keep papers, laptops and phones unreadable or out of frame.
- **Client meetings**: photograph only with the client's agreement, and usually staged rather than real.
- **Seminar guests**: don't publish identifiable clients without consent.
- **Gallery access**: keep galleries private and limited to the marketing team.

## Publicity rules

Many professions have rules on how firms may publicise themselves. **Check your firm's publicity and marketing guidelines**, and any rules that apply to your profession, before publishing photos, captions or award claims. Your risk or compliance team can confirm.

## FAQ

### How do law firms keep partner portraits consistent?
By agreeing a standard background, lighting, crop, pose and retouching level, recording the setup, and photographing new partners and associates with the same setup later.

### What should be photographed at a professional services seminar?
Speakers and panels, the audience engaged without making any single client the focus without consent, and networking between partners and guests who agree, with no confidential slides in frame.

### How do professional services firms protect confidentiality in photos?
By keeping client names, whiteboards, screens, documents and devices out of frame or unreadable, staging client meeting photos, not publishing identifiable clients without consent, and keeping galleries private.

### Are there rules on how law firms use photos in marketing?
Many professions have rules on firm publicity. Check your firm's publicity and marketing guidelines, and any rules for your profession, before publishing photos, captions or award claims.

### Can portraits and an event be photographed on the same day?
Yes. Many firms combine a portrait session in a meeting room with a seminar or office shoot, so the photographer covers both in one visit.
