# University and Polytechnic Event Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Universities, polytechnics, ITE and private institutions in Singapore run events all year, from **open houses** and **orientation** to **public lectures**, **research showcases** and **alumni nights**. Photography for them should:

- show **real students** and campus life, not just stages and booths
- cover **large, spread-out events** with several photographers or fixed points
- respect **student consent**, especially for marketing use
- feed a **photo library** for recruitment, websites and reports

Most institutions also need photos of **leaders, faculty and guests of honour** at key moments.

## Open houses

Open houses are the biggest recruitment events of the year and are spread across a whole campus.

- **Several photographers or fixed points**: key booths, demonstration areas, talks and the campus tour route.
- **Real engagement**: students explaining projects, visitors trying demonstrations, parents asking questions.
- **Crowds and atmosphere**: wide shots at peak times.
- **Each school or faculty**: a list of booths to cover, so every department gets photos.
- **Same-day selects** for social media during the open house weekend. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "same-day event photo delivery"].

## Students and consent

- **Tell students** about photography at events and in student communications.
- **Offer an opt-out**, and respect it.
- **Get written consent** from students who will appear prominently in marketing, such as brochures, ads and banners.
- **Minors**: some students at polytechnics, ITE and pre-university programmes are under 18. Follow the institution's rules on parental consent.
- **Labs and research**: avoid confidential data, screens and unpublished results.

## Building a campus photo library

- **Plan a library shoot** across a few days each year: campus life, labs, facilities and students in each faculty.
- **Show diversity**: students of different backgrounds, courses and ages.
- **Horizontal and vertical** versions for web, social media and print.
- **Space for text** for brochures and banners.
- **Refresh regularly**: new buildings, facilities and uniforms date photos quickly.

## Procurement

Publicly funded institutions usually engage vendors through **quotations or tenders**, often for a **period contract** covering many events in the year. Quotations should state coverage, deliverables, delivery times and usage rights. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/government-event-photography-singapore "government event photography in Singapore"].

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a university or polytechnic open house?
Crowds and atmosphere at peak times, students explaining projects, visitors trying demonstrations, talks, campus tours and every faculty's booth, with same-day selects for social media.

### How many photographers does an open house need?
Usually several, or fixed points at key booths, demonstration areas, talks and the tour route, because open houses are spread across a whole campus.

### Do universities need student consent for marketing photos?
Tell students about photography, offer an opt-out, and get written consent from students who appear prominently in marketing. Follow the institution's rules on parental consent for students under 18.

### How do institutions build a campus photo library?
With a planned shoot each year covering campus life, labs, facilities and students in each faculty, showing diversity, in horizontal and vertical versions with space for text, refreshed as facilities change.
