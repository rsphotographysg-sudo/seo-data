# Corporate Family Day Photography in Singapore: A Planning Guide

**Short answer:** A company family day is spread out, busy and mostly outdoors. To get photos that staff will love:

- **Plan one photographer per busy area**, or at least one roaming photographer for every 300–400 guests.
- **Set up a family photo spot** with your branding, so every family leaves with a good portrait.
- **Agree how children's photos are handled** before the day.
- **Plan for Singapore weather**: shade for the crew, and a rain plan.
- **Share photos quickly**, ideally within a few days, while staff are still talking about the event.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph family days, carnivals, team-building and staff engagement events in Singapore.*

---

## What makes family days different

- **Many things happen at once.** Game booths, bouncy castles, food stalls, performances and lucky draws run in parallel across a large area.
- **Children are everywhere.** Families expect great photos of their kids, but children's photos need extra care.
- **It's usually outdoors.** Parks, resorts, beaches, sports clubs and attractions mean harsh midday sun, heat and the chance of sudden rain.
- **Staff want photos of their own family,** not just the stage.

## How many photographers do you need?

| Guests (staff + families) | Photographers | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 300 | 1–2 | One roaming; add one for a family photo spot |
| 300–800 | 2–3 | Roaming coverage, stage programme and a family photo spot |
| 800+ | 3 or more | Assign zones, plus a dedicated family photo spot |

A **family photo spot** with a branded backdrop and one photographer is often the most popular part of the day. Families queue, get a proper portrait together, and the company gets a gallery of happy staff with their families.

## Children and consent

Children's photos are the ones families value most and the ones that need the most care.

- **Tell families in advance** that photography will take place, in the invitation and on signage on the day.
- **Offer an opt-out.** For example, a coloured sticker for families who don't want their children photographed or published. Brief every photographer on it.
- **Keep galleries private.** Use a password-protected gallery for staff, not a public link.
- **Choose public photos carefully.** For the company's social media or website, use photos where children aren't the main focus, or get the parents' permission.
- **Crew conduct.** Photographers photograph children only in public areas, with parents nearby, and never ask children to go somewhere alone.

## Outdoor events: heat, light and rain

- **Midday sun** creates harsh shadows and squinting faces. Put the family photo spot in **open shade** or under a tent, and use fill flash.
- **Heat** affects both guests and crew. Plan water, shade and breaks for photographers on long outdoor days.
- **Rain** is always possible in Singapore. Agree on a wet-weather plan: covered areas, the indoor backup venue and how the programme changes.
- **Golden hour** in the late afternoon gives the best light for group and family photos if the event runs that long.

## Staff engagement and team-building events

The same planning works for team-building days, sports days and staff carnivals:

- **Photograph every team,** especially in competitions, so no team feels left out.
- **Capture the activity, not just the result**: concentration, teamwork, cheering and the winning moment.
- **Plan the group photo early,** before people start leaving.
- **Coordinate with facilitators** on activity timing, so photographers are at the best moments.

## Sharing photos with staff

Family day photos are shared widely, so make it easy:

- **A private gallery** organised by area or activity, so families can find their photos.
- **Family portraits in their own folder,** ideally named or numbered by the queue order if the gallery is large.
- **Fast delivery.** A highlights set within a day or two, and the full gallery soon after, while the event is fresh.
- **Downloads in web size** for staff, and full resolution for HR or internal comms.

## FAQ

### How many photographers do we need for a company family day?
Plan one roaming photographer for every 300 to 400 guests, and add a dedicated photographer for a family photo spot. Above 800 guests, assign photographers to zones.

### What is a family photo spot?
A branded backdrop in a shaded area, with one photographer taking portraits of each family. It is often the most popular part of a family day and gives the company a gallery of staff with their families.

### How should we handle photos of children at a family day?
Tell families in advance, offer an opt-out such as a coloured sticker, brief all photographers, keep galleries private, and get parents' permission before using children's photos publicly.

### What if it rains at an outdoor family day?
Agree on a wet-weather plan before the day: covered areas, an indoor backup venue and how the programme changes. Photographers should bring rain protection for equipment and know where to move the family photo spot.

### How quickly should family day photos be shared?
A highlights set within one or two days and the full gallery soon after, through a private gallery organised by area or activity, while staff are still talking about the event.
