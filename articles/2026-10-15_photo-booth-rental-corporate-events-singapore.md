# Photo Booth Rental for Corporate Events in Singapore

**Short answer:** A photo booth works best at corporate events where guests have time to relax: **annual dinners, family days, launches and conference networking sessions**.

When comparing options, check:

- the **booth type**
- whether **prints are included** and how many
- whether the **print frame can carry your branding**
- how guests **get digital copies**
- how long the booth runs, and whether an **attendant** is included

At RS Photography, photo booths with instant prints start from **S$800**. Plan about **3 m × 3 m of floor space** and **one power point** near the booth.

## Why corporate events use photo booths

- **Guests take something home.** A printed photo with your logo stays on desks and fridges long after the event.
- **It keeps guests busy** during long dinner courses, breaks and networking.
- **It creates content.** Hundreds of fun, informal photos for internal comms and social media.
- **It lifts sponsor visibility.** Branded frames and backdrops put sponsors in every print.
- **It brings people together.** Teams who rarely meet often queue and pose together.

## Types of photo booth

| Type | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Open-air booth** | A camera, light and backdrop in an open space, no enclosure | Groups, larger events, branded backdrops |
| **Enclosed booth** | A small booth with a curtain | Small groups, a more private and playful feel |
| **Roaming photographer with printer** | A photographer moves around the event and prints on the spot | Cocktail receptions, networking events, VIP areas |
| **GIF and boomerang booth** | Short animated clips instead of, or as well as, prints | Social media-heavy events |
| **360° video booth** | Guests stand on a platform while a camera circles them | Launches, youth audiences, high-energy events |

Not every vendor offers every type. Ask what is available for your date and space.

## What to check before you book

1. **How long does the booth run?** Agree on the hours, for example 3 hours during dinner, and the cost of extra time.
2. **Are prints unlimited?** Some packages include unlimited prints during the booth hours. Others set a limit.
3. **Print size and layout.** Common choices are a 4R print or a photo strip. Ask to see the layout.
4. **Branding.** Can the print frame and on-screen overlay carry your logo, event name and date? Can sponsors be added?
5. **Backdrop and props.** Is a backdrop included? Can it be branded? Are props included, and can they match your theme?
6. **Digital copies.** Will all photos go into an online gallery? Can guests get their photo on their phone straight away?
7. **Attendant.** Is someone there the whole time to help guests, run the printer and keep the queue moving?
8. **Setup time.** How long before the event does the team need access?

## Space, power and placement

- **Space:** plan about **3 m × 3 m** for an open-air booth, including the backdrop, camera and a small queue area. More space is needed for large groups or a 360° booth.
- **Power:** one power point near the booth.
- **Placement:** visible, but not blocking walkways, exits or the stage. Near the bar or entrance works well. Avoid placing it next to speakers or in direct sunlight.
- **Lighting:** booths bring their own light, but avoid placing the backdrop against a window or bright LED wall.

Ask the venue about floor plans and power early. Ballroom layouts fill up fast.

## Branding the prints

The print frame is the most visible piece of branding at many events. Good frames:

- show the **logo and event name** clearly without covering faces
- use **brand colours** that suit both dark and bright photos
- include the **date**, so the print becomes a keepsake
- leave space for a **sponsor logo** if needed

Send logo files and brand guidelines at least a week before the event, so the frame can be designed and approved.

---

## Guest data and privacy

Some booths share photos by asking guests for an **email address or phone number**, or by sending them to a QR code page.

- **Only collect what you need.** A QR code to download the photo often works without collecting any personal data.
- **If you collect emails or numbers,** tell guests what they will be used for, and don't add them to marketing lists without consent.
- **Keep the online gallery private** for internal events.
- **Respect opt-outs.** Guests choose to use the booth, but photos of other guests in the background still need care.

## Combining a booth with event photography

A photo booth is not a replacement for an event photographer. The booth captures guests posing for fun. The photographer captures:

- speeches and the stage programme
- awards and table photos
- candid moments

## FAQ

### How much does a photo booth cost for a corporate event in Singapore?
At RS Photography, photo booths with instant prints start from S$800. The price depends on booth hours, print limits, custom backdrops and extra booth types such as GIF or 360-degree video.

### How much space does a photo booth need?
Plan about 3 by 3 metres for an open-air booth, including the backdrop, camera and a small queue area, plus one power point. Larger groups or a 360-degree booth need more space.

### Can the photo booth prints carry our company logo?
Yes. The print frame and on-screen overlay can usually show your logo, event name and date, and a sponsor logo if needed. Send logo files and brand guidelines at least a week before the event.

### Do guests get digital copies of photo booth pictures?
Usually yes, through an online gallery or a QR code that lets guests download their photo on their phone. A QR code often avoids collecting personal data.

### Do we still need a photographer if we have a photo booth?
Usually yes. The booth captures posed fun photos. A photographer captures speeches, awards, table photos and candid moments. Booking both from one team keeps the plan and branding consistent.
