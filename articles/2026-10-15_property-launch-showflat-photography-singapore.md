# Property Launch and Showflat Event Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** A property launch in Singapore usually has several moments to photograph, each with a different purpose:

- **the showflat itself**: interiors, the model and the gallery, before visitors arrive
- **the VIP or agents' preview**: briefings, the developer's leadership and key partners
- **the launch weekend**: crowds, queues and engaged visitors, which show demand
- **booking day**: activity and atmosphere, while **protecting buyers' privacy**

Agree with the developer and marketing agency **which photos will be used publicly**, and keep buyers' faces, names and documents out of anything published without consent.

## What to photograph at each stage

| Stage | What to photograph | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Before opening** | Showflat interiors, the scale model, the sales gallery, signage | Marketing, website, brochures |
| **Agents' briefing** | Developer presentations, agents in the gallery, Q&A | Internal comms, partner updates |
| **VIP or media preview** | Leadership, guests of honour, the model, tours of the showflat | Press, social media |
| **Launch weekend** | Crowds, queues, families viewing the model, consultations | Social media, launch updates |
| **Booking day** | Atmosphere, the hall, staff at work; no identifiable buyers without consent | Internal records, carefully selected social posts |

## Showflat interiors

Showflat photography is closer to **interior and architectural photography** than event photography:

- **Photograph before visitors arrive**, when the units are clean and staged.
- **Use a tripod** and careful lighting, so rooms look bright and true to colour.
- **Keep lines straight**: vertical walls should look vertical.
- **Show the layout**: wide shots of each room plus details of finishes and fittings.
- **Check accuracy**: photos should represent the unit fairly. Follow the developer's guidance on how showflat photos are captioned and used.

## The scale model and sales gallery

- **The model**: overall views and close-ups of key features, lit to show detail.
- **People with the model**: visitors pointing, agents explaining. These show interest better than the model alone.
- **Gallery displays**: material boards, digital screens and brand signage.

## Launch weekend: showing demand

- **Wide shots** of a busy gallery at peak times
- **Queues and registrations**, photographed so no one's personal details are readable
- **Families and couples** viewing the model and the units (with consent if they are the focus)
- **Agents at work**: consultations and explanations

## Booking day and buyer privacy

Booking day is sensitive. Buyers are making a major financial decision, often sharing personal and financial information.

- **Agree the rules first** with the developer: which areas may be photographed, and how photos will be used.
- **No readable documents**: forms, cheques, NRICs, screens and price lists with personal details must not appear in photos.
- **No identifiable buyers** in public-facing photos without their consent.
- **Focus on atmosphere**: the hall, staff, wide views and the developer's team.
- **Keep the gallery private** and limited to the client.

- **Who is the client?** The developer, the marketing agency or the appointed real estate agency. Agree who receives and approves the photos.

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a property launch in Singapore?
The showflat interiors, scale model and sales gallery before opening, the agents' briefing, the VIP or media preview, crowds and engaged visitors on the launch weekend, and the atmosphere on booking day without identifiable buyers.

### How should showflat interiors be photographed?
Before visitors arrive, with a tripod and careful lighting, straight vertical lines, wide shots of each room and details of finishes. Photos should represent the unit fairly and follow the developer's guidance on captions and use.

### Can buyers be photographed on booking day?
Only with care. Agree rules with the developer first, keep forms, cheques, IDs and screens out of frame, and don't use identifiable buyers in public-facing photos without their consent.

### How do you show demand in launch photos?
With wide shots of a busy gallery at peak times, queues photographed without readable personal details, visitors engaging with the model, and agents in consultation.

### How is property launch photography priced?
Usually by session or day: showflat interiors before opening, the preview event, and peak sessions on the launch weekend, with same-day delivery for social media if needed.
