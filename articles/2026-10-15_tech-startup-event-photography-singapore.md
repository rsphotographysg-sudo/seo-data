# Tech Conference and Startup Event Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Tech conferences, startup pitches, hackathons and demo days move fast and live on social media. Plan for four things:

- **LED-wall stages**: expose for speakers' faces, not the screen
- **Live demos and product moments**: the screen and the person in one frame
- **People**: founders, investors, builders and their reactions
- **Speed**: content posted during the event, often within the hour

Sponsor activations and exhibitor booths need their own coverage. Overnight hackathons need a plan for **shifts and low light**.

## LED walls and stage lighting

Tech stages often use large **LED walls** with bright, moving graphics.

- **Expose for faces.** If the camera exposes for the bright screen, speakers look dark. Expose for faces and let the screen go a little bright.
- **Ask the AV team for help.** A little more front light on speakers makes a big difference.
- **Watch for screen patterns.** LED walls can show lines or flicker in photos at some shutter speeds. Experienced photographers adjust for this.
- **Capture the scale.** A wide shot from the back shows the stage, the screen and the full audience. It is often the most used photo of the event.

## Live demos and product moments

- **Get the screen and the person in one frame.** A demo photo works when viewers can see what is happening on screen and who is doing it.
- **Ask for the demo schedule**, so the photographer is in place.
- **Photograph hands-on moments**: attendees trying devices, apps and VR headsets.
- **Avoid confidential screens.** Unreleased products and internal tools should be agreed in advance.

## Pitch competitions and demo days

- **Every founder on stage**: photograph each pitch, with the company name or slide visible.
- **Judges and investors**: reactions and questions.
- **Winners and awards**: the announcement moment and a group photo.
- **Founder portraits**: a quick portrait station gives every startup a professional photo for its press kit and LinkedIn. It is a popular add-on.

Name photos by company, so organisers and founders can find theirs quickly. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/award-ceremony-photography-singapore "award ceremony photography"] for naming photos per recipient.

## Hackathons

Hackathons often run overnight or over a weekend.

- **Cover in shifts**: kick-off, a late-night session, and the final presentations and judging, rather than continuous coverage.
- **Low light**: work with available light and discreet flash. Don't disturb teams that are working.
- **Tell the story**: whiteboards, laptops, mentors, food, tired faces at 3 am and the winning moment.
- **Team photos**: one per team, for the organiser's recap and the teams' own posts.

## FAQ

### How do you photograph speakers in front of an LED wall?
Expose for the speakers' faces rather than the bright screen, ask the AV team for more front light, adjust settings to avoid screen lines or flicker, and take wide shots that show the stage, screen and audience together.

### What should we photograph at a pitch competition?
Every founder on stage with the company name or slide visible, judges' and investors' reactions, the winners' announcement and a group photo. A founder portrait station is a popular add-on.

### How do you photograph an overnight hackathon?
In shifts: the kick-off, a late-night session and the final presentations and judging. Use available light and discreet flash, avoid disturbing teams, and take a photo of every team.

### Can we post tech event photos during the event?
Yes. With an on-site editor, selected photos can be ready within the hour, with vertical crops for stories and short clips for reels.

### How do we avoid photographing confidential screens at tech events?
Agree in advance which demos and products may be shown, and keep laptops and screens with internal tools or unreleased products out of frame.
