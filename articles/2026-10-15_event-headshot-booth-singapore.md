# Headshot Booths at Conferences and Events in Singapore

**Short answer:** A headshot booth gives every attendee a **professional profile photo** at a conference, career fair or company event. It is one of the most popular activations because attendees use the photo on LinkedIn for years. To run one well:

- **Space**: about **3 m × 3 m**, away from noisy, busy walkways, with power
- **Throughput**: around **one person every two to three minutes** per photographer, so plan queues or booking slots
- **Branding**: the event or **sponsor's logo** on the booth and on the delivery message
- **Delivery**: retouched photos sent within a day, or quick selects on the spot
- **Consent**: collect email addresses with clear consent for delivery

## Space and setup

- **About 3 m × 3 m**, with a backdrop, lights and a small waiting area
- **A quiet corner**, not in the main walkway, so people feel comfortable
- **Power** near the setup
- **A mirror and a small grooming table** (comb, tissues, blotting paper)
- **Signage** with the booth name, times and how delivery works

---

## Throughput: how many people?

- **About 20 to 30 people an hour** per photographer, at one person every two to three minutes, including a quick look at the photos. That is faster than a planned staff headshot session (about 10–12 people an hour), because each person gets fewer looks and less coaching.
- **Peaks**: queues build at breaks and lunch. A **second photographer** or a **booking system** keeps waits short.
- **Booking slots**: attendees book a time on the event app or a sign-up sheet.
- **Walk-ins**: allow some free slots for people who see the booth.

## Consistent, flattering results

- **A simple background**, usually plain grey, white or a soft brand colour
- **Soft, even lighting** that flatters every face
- **Quick coaching**: a few words on posture and expression
- **Several frames** per person, so they can choose the best
- **Light retouching**: natural, not heavy

## Sponsor value

A headshot booth is an effective sponsor activation:

- **The sponsor's logo** on the booth, the backdrop edge or signage (not on the photo itself, so attendees will use it)
- **The delivery email** branded "with compliments of" the sponsor
- **Sponsor staff** at the booth to talk to attendees while they wait
- **Reporting**: the number of people photographed, for the sponsor's report

---

## Delivery

- **On the spot**: attendees choose a frame on a screen or tablet.
- **Within a day**: retouched photos sent by email or a private download link.
- **Sizes**: a square crop for LinkedIn and a full-resolution version.

## Consent and email addresses

Collecting email addresses for delivery means handling personal data:

- **Explain** what the email address will be used for: photo delivery, and anything else only if the person agrees.
- **Get separate consent** before sharing emails with sponsors for marketing.
- **Keep data secure** and delete it after delivery if it isn't needed.

## FAQ

### How many people can a headshot booth photograph in an hour?
About 20 to 30 people per photographer per hour, at one person every two to three minutes. A second photographer or booking slots help at breaks and lunch.

### How much space does a headshot booth need?
About 3 m by 3 m in a quiet corner, with power, a backdrop, lights, a mirror and a small waiting area.

### How are headshots from an event booth delivered?
Attendees can choose a frame on a screen at the booth, and retouched photos are sent within a day by email or a private link, in a square LinkedIn crop and full resolution.

### Can a sponsor brand a headshot booth?
Yes. The sponsor's logo can appear on the booth and signage, and the delivery email can be branded with the sponsor's compliments, while the photo itself stays unbranded so attendees use it.

### Do we need consent to collect emails at a headshot booth?
Yes. Explain that the email is for photo delivery, get separate consent before sharing it with sponsors for marketing, keep the data secure, and delete it when no longer needed.
