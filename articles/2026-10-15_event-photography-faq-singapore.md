# Corporate Event Photography FAQ: Singapore

Short answers to the questions organisers ask us most, with a link to the detailed guide for each topic.

*Answered by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. RS Photography has photographed corporate, MICE and government events in Singapore since 2013.*

For the full overview, see [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-event-photography-singapore-guide "Corporate event photography in Singapore: the complete guide"].

## Prices and booking

### How much does corporate event photography cost in Singapore?
Typically S$600–S$1,200 for a half day and S$1,500–S$2,800 for a full day with one photographer. Gala dinners and MICE events usually run S$2,000–S$3,500 or more.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-event-photography-cost-singapore "Event photography costs"]

### Is GST included?
It depends on the vendor. RS Photography is not GST-registered, so our quotations are nett and final.

### How far ahead should we book?
Four to eight weeks for most events. Book earlier for November to January, the busiest season for annual dinners and year-end events. Short-notice bookings are often still possible on weekdays.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/last-minute-event-photographer-singapore "Short-notice bookings"]

### Do we need a professional, or can staff use phones?
Phones are enough for small, bright, informal internal events. A professional is worth it for low light, stages, VIPs, large events, and photos for press, reports or ads.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/professional-event-photographer-vs-smartphone "Professional or phone"]

### How do we choose a photographer?
Ask for full galleries from events like yours, confirm backup photographers and equipment, and get delivery times and usage rights in writing.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/how-to-choose-corporate-event-photographer-singapore "How to choose an event photographer"]

## Planning

### How many photographers do we need?
One for each area that runs at the same time. For dinners, plan about one per 200–250 guests.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/conference-photography-checklist "Conference checklist"] · [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/annual-dinner-photography-singapore "Annual dinners"]

### What should we send the photographer before the event?
The run-of-show, a shot list, the speaker and VIP list with headshots, venue details and one day-of contact, at least a week before.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/event-photography-brief-template "Brief template"]

### How do we get a good large group photo?
Give it a fixed time in the programme (not the end), a location with a raised camera position, VIPs in the centre of the front row, and helpers to bring people in.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/large-group-photo-corporate-event-singapore "Large group photos"]

### What if our outdoor event gets rained out?
Agree a wet-weather plan before the day: covered areas, an indoor backup, a decision time and one person who decides. Pause outdoor activities if there is lightning.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/outdoor-event-photography-singapore-weather "Outdoor events"]

### Why do speakers look dark in event photos?
Because the screen behind them is brighter than their faces. Ask the AV team for more front light on speakers and to lower the screen brightness during speeches.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/event-stage-lighting-backdrop-photos "Stage lighting and backdrops"]

### How early does the photographer arrive?
Usually 45–60 minutes before doors open, to check the venue and lighting.

## Delivery

### How fast do we get the photos?
A preview set within 24–48 hours and the full gallery within 3–5 working days.

### How many photos will we get?
As a rough guide, around 50 to 150 edited photos per photographer per hour, depending on the event. Detailed retouching and RAW files are usually extra.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/how-many-event-photos-editing "Photos and editing"]

### Can we get photos during the event?
Yes. With an on-site editor, first selects can arrive within the hour.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "Same-day delivery"]

### What should we do with the photos afterwards?
Post during the event and the next day, send speakers and sponsors their photos, build a recap page within a week, and reuse the photos for next year's event and the annual report.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/how-to-use-corporate-event-photos "Using event photos"]

### Who owns the photos?
Whatever the quotation and contract say. If they say nothing, under the Copyright Act 2021 the photographer is usually the default owner. Confirm usage rights before booking, especially for advertising or government contracts.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/event-photo-copyright-usage-rights-singapore "Copyright and usage rights"]

## Privacy and consent

### Does the PDPA apply to event photos?
Photos of identifiable people are personal data. Tell guests photography is taking place, offer an opt-out, and deliver photos securely.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/pdpa-event-photography-consent-guide "PDPA consent guide"]

### How do we handle guests who don't want to be photographed?
Give them a visible marker, such as a coloured lanyard, and brief every photographer on it.

### What about children?
Tell families in advance, offer an opt-out, keep galleries private, and get parents' agreement before using children's photos publicly.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/school-event-photography-singapore "School events"] · [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-family-day-photography-singapore "Family days"]

## Government and public sector

### Can government agencies engage RS Photography?
Yes, through GeBIZ or the agency's procurement platform. Work starts after a Purchase Order is issued.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/government-event-photography-singapore "Government events"]

### Is bizSAFE required?
It depends on the quotation documents. Level 3 is a common requirement for on-site work. RS Photography is bizSAFE Level 3 certified.

### Do you photograph embassy and national day receptions?
Yes. The key is the receiving line: one photographer stays with the ambassador to photograph every guest, while a second covers the room, following protocol and precedence.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/embassy-diplomatic-reception-photography-singapore "Embassy events"]

### How do you photograph a ministerial visit or facility tour?
Walk the route beforehand, stay ahead of the group at every stop, photograph the VIP interacting with staff, follow PPE and security rules, and deliver captioned photos the same day.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/vip-visit-facility-tour-photography-singapore "VIP visits"]

### What should be photographed at a community centre event?
The guest of honour's arrival, walkabout and speech, residents of all ages at the activities, volunteers and partners, and group photos with the committee.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/grassroots-community-event-photography-singapore "Community events"]

### Does the PDPA apply to vendors working for government agencies?
Yes. Since 1 February 2021, vendors acting for public agencies are no longer excluded from the PDPA.

## Types of event

### How do we photograph every award winner?
Use stage marks, a pause facing the camera, and a running order to name photos per recipient.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/award-ceremony-photography-singapore "Award ceremonies"]

### When should we plan photos for a company anniversary?
Three to six months before the celebration, to photograph founders and long-serving staff, collect archive photos and design a book or video.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/company-anniversary-celebration-photography-singapore "Company anniversaries"]

### How do we photograph lo hei and a lion dance?
Find out exactly when each starts, place the photographer before the moment, start with the main table for lo hei, and get the lion's route and the plucking of the greens in advance.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/festive-corporate-celebration-photography-singapore "Festive celebrations"]

### How do you photograph a team building race across the city?
With photographers at two or three checkpoints every team passes, a roving photographer between them, and the start and finish covered. Photograph every team at least once.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/team-building-offsite-photography-singapore "Team building"]

### How long does a photographer need at a seminar or workshop?
Usually one to two hours, covering the opening, activities and the closing. Several sessions in one day can be combined into one booking.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/seminar-workshop-training-photography-singapore "Seminars and training"]

### What should be photographed at a golf day?
Every flight before tee-off, the flag-off, sponsor and contest holes, players in action without disturbing play, and every winner at the prize dinner.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-golf-day-photography-singapore "Golf days"]

### Can buyers be photographed at a property launch?
Only with care. Keep forms, cheques, IDs and screens out of frame, and don't use identifiable buyers in public photos without consent. Focus on the showflat, the model, crowds and the developer's team.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/property-launch-showflat-photography-singapore "Property launches"]

### When should food be photographed at a restaurant launch?
Before guests arrive, with the chef plating each dish fresh. Then photograph the space, the chef and the event itself.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/restaurant-launch-media-tasting-photography-singapore "Restaurant launches"]

### Can performers at our event be photographed?
Usually, but check the performer's agreement first: some limit photos, video or flash, or need approval before publishing.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/stage-performance-photography-corporate-events-singapore "Stage performances"]

### How do you photograph a university or polytechnic open house?
With several photographers or fixed points across the campus, covering every faculty's booth, demonstrations, talks and tours, with same-day selects for social media.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/university-polytechnic-event-photography-singapore "Universities and polytechnics"]

### When is the best time to photograph an exhibition booth?
The empty booth before doors open, and the busy booth at peak hours.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/exhibition-booth-photography-singapore "Exhibitions"]

### Does a roadshow photographer need to stay all day?
Usually not. Photograph the setup before opening, then peak hours and key moments. For multi-day roadshows, two or three peak sessions are often enough.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/roadshow-brand-activation-photography-singapore "Roadshows"]

### How do we get a good ribbon-cutting or groundbreaking photo?
Rehearse, mark VIP positions, reserve a central camera spot, and ask VIPs to hold facing the camera.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/groundbreaking-opening-ceremony-photography-singapore "Ceremonies"]

### How do we get press-ready photos from a launch?
Rehearse the unveil, prepare names and titles, and send a small captioned set within hours.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/product-launch-press-event-photography-singapore "Launches and press events"]

## Video, streaming and other services

### Should we book photo and video from one company?
For most events, yes. You get one brief, one contact and a consistent style, and it usually costs less.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/event-photography-and-videography-singapore "Photo and video"]

### What do we need to live stream an event?
A wired connection with a mobile backup, audio from the AV desk, slides taken directly from the source, a rehearsal, and someone looking after the online audience.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/live-streaming-corporate-events-singapore "Live streaming"]

### How long does a corporate video take?
Usually 4–8 weeks from brief to delivery.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-video-production-singapore "Corporate video"]

### How much does a photo booth cost?
Photo booths with instant prints start from S$800 at RS Photography.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/photo-booth-rental-corporate-events-singapore "Photo booths"]

### How do professional firms keep partner portraits consistent?
Agree a standard background, lighting, crop and retouching level, record the setup, and photograph new partners the same way later.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/law-firm-professional-services-photography-singapore "Professional services"]

### How many headshots can be done in an hour?
About 10–12 people, using 5-minute slots.
→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-headshots-singapore "Corporate headshots"] · [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/workplace-employer-branding-photography-singapore "Workplace photos"]
