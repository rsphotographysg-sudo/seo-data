# How to Use Your Corporate Event Photos: A Post-Event Plan

**Short answer:** Most event photos are used once, in a single social post, and then forgotten. Plan their use **before the event**, and the same gallery can serve you for a year:

- **During the event and the next day**: social posts, speaker and sponsor shares
- **Within a week**: thank-you emails, an event recap page, press and newsletters
- **Within a month**: case studies, sales decks and internal comms
- **All year**: next year's registration page, annual reports, recruitment and your website

Ask your photographer for **folders by use**, **crops for each platform** and **captions with names**, and check **consent** before using guests' photos in ads.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph corporate, MICE and government events in Singapore and help clients plan how the photos will be used.*

## Plan the uses before the event

Tell the photographer where the photos will go. It changes what they shoot:

- **Social media** needs vertical and square crops, and people's reactions.
- **Press** needs a few clean, captioned photos of the key moment.
- **Sponsors** need their logos, booths and representatives.
- **Next year's marketing** needs wide shots of a full room, plus photos with space for text.
- **The annual report** needs leadership on stage and high-resolution files.

## Social media

- **Post while people still care.** Engagement is highest during the event and the day after.
- **Tag speakers, sponsors and partners.** They reshare, which reaches their audiences.
- **Use different crops** for LinkedIn, Instagram feed and stories.
- **Mix photo types**: the full room, the key moment, speakers, and genuine reactions.
- **Spread the gallery out.** One recap post, then a few more over the following weeks rather than 50 photos at once.

---

## Thank-you emails and the event recap page

- **Send the thank-you email within a week**, with three to five photos and a link to the gallery.
- **Create an event recap page** on your website with photos, highlights, speakers and key numbers. It becomes the page that people find when they search for next year's event.
- **Link the recap page** from next year's registration page.

---

## Speakers, sponsors and partners

- **Speakers**: send each speaker their own photos within a day or two. Most will post them and promote your event.
- **Sponsors**: send a sponsor folder with logos, booths and their representatives. It is a simple way to show value and help renewals.
- **Partners and VIPs**: send the key moment and group photos with captions.

---

## Press and newsletters

- **Choose a few clean photos** of the key moment, captioned with names and titles.
- **Send them with the press release** on the day, or the morning after.
- **Use the gallery** in your next newsletter and in your industry association's newsletter.

## Consent and usage rights

- **Check usage rights** in the photographer's quotation: social media, website, print, press and paid advertising.
- **Be careful with paid ads.** Using an identifiable guest's photo in an advertisement needs more care than a recap post. Get consent, or use photos of speakers, staff and crowds where no one is the focus.
- **Respect opt-outs**: remove photos of anyone who asked not to be photographed.
- **Children**: use their photos publicly only with parents' agreement.

## Help your photos get found

When you put event photos on your website:

- **Rename files** to describe the photo, for example `annual-dinner-2026-award-winners.jpg`, not `IMG_4821.jpg`.
- **Write alt text** that describes the photo for people who can't see it.
- **Compress large files**, so the page loads fast.
- **Add a caption** with the event name, date and place.

These help search engines and AI assistants understand what the page is about.

## FAQ

### What should we do with event photos after the event?
Post on social media during the event and the next day, send speakers and sponsors their photos, include photos in a thank-you email and an event recap page within a week, then use them in case studies, sales decks, internal comms, next year's registration page and the annual report.

### How soon should we post corporate event photos?
During the event and the day after, when engagement is highest. With an on-site editor, selected photos can be ready within the hour, and a preview set within 24 to 48 hours.

### How do we get speakers and sponsors to share our event photos?
Send each speaker their own photos within a day or two, send sponsors a folder with their logos, booths and representatives, and tag them in your posts.

### Can we use guests' event photos in paid advertising?
Take more care than for a recap post. Get consent from identifiable guests, check the photographer's usage rights, and prefer photos of speakers, staff and crowds where no one is the focus.

### How do event photos help SEO?
On your website, descriptive file names, alt text, captions with the event name and date, and compressed files help search engines and AI assistants understand the page. An event recap page can also rank for searches about next year's event.
