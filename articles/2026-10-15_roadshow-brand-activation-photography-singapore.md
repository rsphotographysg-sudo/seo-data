# Roadshow and Brand Activation Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Roadshows, pop-ups and brand activations in Singapore's malls and public spaces are photographed to **prove they worked**. Plan coverage around:

- **peak hours**, when the crowd and engagement are at their best
- **shoppers interacting** with the product, games, sampling and staff
- **branding**: the booth, logos and key messages clearly visible
- **evidence for reports**: crowds, queues, sign-ups and redemptions, with dates and locations

For **multi-day or multi-location** roadshows, photograph **selected peak sessions** at each site rather than every hour, and agree the **mall's photography rules** in advance.

## What brands and agencies need

| Need | What to photograph |
|---|---|
| **Proof of execution** | The booth fully set up, branding, signage and staff in uniform, before opening |
| **Engagement** | Shoppers trying products, playing games, taking samples, talking to staff |
| **Crowds and queues** | Wide shots at peak hours showing traffic and interest |
| **Results** | Sign-ups, redemptions, purchases and prize presentations |
| **Content for social media** | Fun reactions, influencers or hosts, short video clips |
| **Partners and sponsors** | Partner logos, mall partnership signage, representatives on site |

Agencies often need these photos for the **client's post-campaign report**. Ask what the report needs before the shoot.

---

## Timing: photograph the peaks

A roadshow running 10 am to 10 pm doesn't need 12 hours of photography.

- **Before opening**: the clean, fully set-up booth with no crowds, for proof of execution.
- **Peak hours**: lunchtime and evenings on weekdays, afternoons on weekends, or during stage programmes and games.
- **Key moments**: the opening, a celebrity or host appearance, a lucky draw or a product demo.

For a multi-day roadshow, **choose two or three peak sessions** across the run, such as the opening day, a weekday evening and a weekend afternoon.

---

## Multi-location roadshows

Roadshows often move across several malls or MRT-linked spaces.

- **Photograph the same set of shots at every location**, so the report compares like with like.
- **Name folders by location and date**.
- **Plan travel time** between sites, especially across the island.
- **Note what differs**: booth size, traffic and setup at each site.

---

## Mall and venue rules

- **Ask the mall's management** about photography and video rules in advance. Some require a permit or notice, especially for tripods, lighting or drones.
- **Stay within your booth area** and don't block walkways, escalators or other tenants' shopfronts.
- **No large lighting setups** in public areas unless approved.
- **Respect shoppers**: see privacy below.

---

## Shoppers and privacy

Roadshows photograph members of the public:

- **Put up signage** at the booth saying that photography and video are taking place.
- **Ask before close-ups**: a shopper trying a product should agree before they become the face of your campaign.
- **Get written consent** for anyone who will appear prominently in ads or social media.
- **Take extra care with children**: use their photos only with parents' agreement.
- **Wide crowd shots** where no one is the focus are lower risk for reports.

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a roadshow or brand activation?
The fully set-up booth before opening, shoppers interacting with products, games and staff, crowds and queues at peak hours, sign-ups, redemptions and prize presentations, branding and partner logos, and fun reactions for social media.

### Does a roadshow photographer need to stay all day?
Usually not. Photograph the setup before opening, then peak hours and key moments such as the opening, a host appearance or a lucky draw. For multi-day roadshows, two or three peak sessions across the run are often enough.

### Do we need the mall's permission to photograph a roadshow?
Check with the mall's management in advance. Some malls require notice or a permit, especially for tripods, lighting or drones, and photographers should stay within the booth area without blocking walkways or other tenants.

### Can we use photos of shoppers in our marketing?
Put up signage that photography is taking place, ask before close-ups, and get written consent from anyone who will appear prominently in ads or social media. Use photos of children only with parents' agreement.

### How should roadshow photos be delivered for client reports?
In folders organised by location, date and shot type, such as setup, engagement, crowds and results, with same-day selects for social media and daily highlights for multi-day roadshows.
