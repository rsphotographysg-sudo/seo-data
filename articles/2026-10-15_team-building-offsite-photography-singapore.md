# Team Building and Corporate Offsite Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Team building photos should show **people working together and having fun**, not only posed group shots. Plan the coverage with the **facilitator's programme**:

- the **briefing** and team formation
- **activities in action**: teamwork, effort and reactions
- **results**: the winning team and prize presentation
- a **photo of every team**, with team names or colours

For activities spread across a city or venue, use **fixed checkpoints** plus a **roving photographer**. For multi-day offsites, agree which sessions are photographed and keep **strategy discussions confidential**.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph team building days, offsites and staff engagement activities in Singapore.*

## Types of team building activity

| Activity | Photography approach |
|---|---|
| **Amazing races and scavenger hunts** | Fixed checkpoints at key stations, a roving photographer, the start and the finish |
| **Workshops** (cooking, terrarium, art jamming, coffee) | Close-ups of hands at work, teams laughing, finished results held up |
| **Sports and outdoor** (kayaking, dragon boat, games) | Action shots from the shore or sidelines, team spirit, the winners |
| **Escape rooms and indoor challenges** | Briefing, team reactions before and after; rooms are often too small and dark for coverage inside |
| **CSR and volunteering** | Teams at work, impact moments; consent from beneficiaries |
| **Offsites and retreats** | Plenary sessions, breakouts, activities, dinner and the closing |

---

## Races and activities across a city

Teams spread out across Singapore or a large venue, so photographers can't follow everyone.

- **Choose fixed checkpoints** where every team passes: two or three stations with interesting tasks or landmarks.
- **Add a roving photographer** to catch teams between stations.
- **Cover the start and the finish**: the energy at the start and the reactions at the finish line.
- **Ask the facilitator for the route** and timings, and share a contact number for the day.
- **Photograph every team at least once**: check off teams by name or colour.

---

## Workshops and indoor activities

- **Hands and details**: ingredients, tools and work in progress.
- **Reactions**: laughter, concentration and help between colleagues.
- **Results**: each team holding up their dish, terrarium or artwork.
- **Light**: workshops are often in small rooms. Use soft flash, bounced off the ceiling where possible.

---

## Outdoor and water activities

- **Waterproof equipment** or a safe position on the shore for kayaking and dragon boat activities.
- **Long lenses** for action from a distance.
- **Heat and rain**: plan shade, water and a wet-weather plan. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/outdoor-event-photography-singapore-weather "outdoor event photography in Singapore weather"].
- **Safety first**: photographers follow the activity provider's instructions and never get in the way of safety staff.

## CSR and volunteering days

Many team building days include **volunteering** with a charity, school or community group.

- **Get consent** from the partner organisation and beneficiaries before photographing them.
- **Focus on your team at work**: packing, building, cleaning, teaching.
- **Show impact with care**: avoid photos that make beneficiaries look helpless.

See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/nonprofit-charity-photography-singapore "non-profit and charity photography"] for more on beneficiary dignity and consent.

## Offsites and retreats

Leadership and company offsites mix work and bonding, often over **one to three days**, in Singapore or overseas.

- **Agree which sessions are photographed.** Plenary sessions and activities usually are. Strategy discussions and breakouts often are not.
- **Keep whiteboards, slides and screens out of frame** if they show confidential plans.
- **Cover the social parts**: arrivals, activities, the dinner, awards and the closing.
- **Leadership photos**: a good opportunity for team photos of the leadership group.
- **Headshot station**: an offsite is a convenient time to update everyone's headshots. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-headshots-singapore "corporate headshots"].

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a team building event?
The briefing and team formation, activities in action showing teamwork and reactions, the results and prize presentation, a photo of every team with their names or colours, and a whole-group photo.

### How do you photograph an amazing race across Singapore?
With fixed photographers at two or three checkpoints that every team passes, a roving photographer between stations, and coverage at the start and the finish. Get the route and timings from the facilitator, and check that every team is photographed.

### Should strategy sessions at an offsite be photographed?
Usually only the plenary parts. Agree in advance which sessions are photographed, and keep whiteboards, slides and screens with confidential plans out of frame.

### How do we photograph a CSR or volunteering day?
Get consent from the partner organisation and beneficiaries first, focus on your team at work, and show impact with care, avoiding photos that make beneficiaries look helpless.

### Can we show team building photos on the same day?
Yes. A same-day slideshow at the closing session or dinner is popular, followed by highlights the next day and folders organised by team.
