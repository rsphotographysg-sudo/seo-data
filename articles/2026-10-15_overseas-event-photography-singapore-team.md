# Overseas Event Photography: Fly a Singapore Team or Hire Local?

**Short answer:** If your organisation is based in Singapore and runs an event in **Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok or further away**, flying your Singapore photography team makes sense when:

- **consistency** with your other events matters
- the event is **high-profile** (leadership, regional conference, ministerial delegation)
- you need **one accountable vendor**

Hiring a local photographer makes sense for **small, simple events** where travel costs would outweigh the benefit.

Either way, plan travel days, equipment, payment and delivery early.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. RS Photography covers events in Singapore and across the region, including Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand and Australia, as well as overseas delegations.*

---

## When to fly a Singapore team

- **Brand consistency.** Your regional summit should look like your Singapore events: same style, colour and coverage.
- **High-stakes events.** Leadership offsites, regional conferences, product launches and official delegations, where a missed moment is costly.
- **Familiarity with your people.** A team that already knows your CEO, your brand guidelines and your comms team needs less briefing and makes fewer mistakes.
- **One contract and one invoice.** Your procurement and finance teams deal with one known vendor, in SGD.
- **Multi-city tours.** One team that follows a roadshow or delegation across several cities gives you a consistent set of photos.

## When to hire locally

- **Small or short events** of an hour or two, where flights and hotels would cost more than the photography.
- **Very tight budgets.**
- **Local-language events** where cultural familiarity matters more than consistency with Singapore events.
- **Remote locations** where travel time is long and the event is simple.

A mixed approach also works: **a Singapore lead photographer** for the main moments, with **local support** for parallel sessions.

## Planning travel and logistics

- **Travel days.** Most teams fly in the day before to check the venue and rest, especially for early-morning starts.
- **Entry and work requirements.** Rules for working on short trips differ by country and change over time. Check with the organiser, venue or local partner well before the trip what the crew needs.
- **Equipment.** Cameras, lenses, lighting and laptops travel as carry-on and checked luggage. Some countries may require customs paperwork for professional equipment, such as an ATA Carnet. Check in advance.
- **Venue access.** Send crew names for passes, as you would at home.
- **Local transport.** Arrange a car or driver between the airport, hotel and venue, especially with heavy equipment.
- **Insurance.** Check that equipment and travel insurance cover the destination.

---

## Delivering photos from overseas

- **Upload speeds** vary. Hotel and venue internet may be slow for large files, so plan a local SIM or mobile data for same-day selects.
- **Time zones.** For events in Australia, Japan or Europe, agree delivery times in Singapore time, so your comms team at home knows when to expect photos.
- **Same-day selects** work overseas too, with an on-site editor. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "same-day event photo delivery"].
- **Full gallery** is usually delivered after the team returns, within the normal turnaround.

## What overseas coverage costs

The photography itself is priced as it would be in Singapore. On top of that, travel costs are added:

- **flights** for each crew member, plus excess baggage for equipment
- **accommodation** near the venue
- **travel days**: time spent travelling, often charged at a reduced rate
- **per diem** for meals and local transport
- **local costs**: a car, a local assistant, or a SIM card for data

For photography prices, see [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-event-photography-cost-singapore "corporate event photography costs in Singapore"].

**Tip:** for regional events within a few hours' flight (Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok), travel often adds less than people expect, especially for multi-day events.

## Delegations and overseas trips

Government and corporate delegations travelling overseas often need a photographer and videographer to travel with them. For delegations:

- **Travel with the delegation schedule**, including site visits, meetings and official dinners.
- **Plan protocol** with the organiser: where the crew may stand at official meetings and signings.
- **Deliver daily** selects for press releases and social media back home.
- **Plan security and registration** for the host country's venues.

## FAQ

### Should we fly our Singapore photographer to an overseas event?
Yes, when consistency with your other events matters, the event is high-profile, or you want one accountable vendor. For small or short events, hiring locally is usually more cost-effective.

### What does overseas event photography cost?
The photography is priced as in Singapore, plus flights, excess baggage, accommodation, travel days, per diem and local costs such as transport and data. For events within a few hours' flight, travel often adds less than expected.

### Does the crew need anything special to work overseas?
Rules for working on short trips and for bringing professional equipment differ by country. Check entry, work and customs requirements, such as an ATA Carnet, with the organiser or local partner well before the trip.

### Can we get same-day photos from an overseas event?
Yes. With an on-site editor and a local SIM or mobile data, selected photos can be delivered on the day. Agree delivery times in Singapore time so your team at home knows when to expect them.

### Can a Singapore photographer travel with a delegation?
Yes. Photographers and videographers can follow the delegation schedule, plan protocol with the organiser, and send daily selects back home for press releases and social media.
