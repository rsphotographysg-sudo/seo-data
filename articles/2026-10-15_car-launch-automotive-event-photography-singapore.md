# Car Launch and Automotive Event Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** A car launch combines **vehicle photography** and **event photography**, and the car is the hardest subject in the room: its glossy paint reflects every light, person and wall. Plan for:

- **hero shots of the car before guests arrive**, when the car is clean and the room is empty
- **the reveal**: the cover coming off, timed with the music and lights
- **VIPs, media and guests** with the car
- **details**: wheels, badges, interior and new features
- **test drives** or **media drives**, if part of the programme

Agree on **lighting, reveal timing and photo positions** with the event agency in advance.

## Hero shots before guests arrive

- **Clean car, empty room**: photograph before doors open, after the car has been detailed.
- **Classic angles**: front three-quarter, side profile, rear three-quarter and the front.
- **Details**: wheels, lights, badges, the interior, the dashboard and new features.
- **Wheels turned** slightly to show the rims, as the brand prefers.
- **Ask the brand** for its photo guidelines: many car brands have specific angles and rules.

---

## Controlling reflections

Car paint acts like a mirror:

- **Soft, large light sources** create smooth reflections along the body.
- **Watch the reflections** of people, walls, cables and signage, and move or remove what you can.
- **A polariser** helps control reflections on glass and paint.
- **Dark clothing** for the photographer, who also appears in the reflections.
- **Check every angle** on screen before guests arrive.

---

## The reveal

The reveal is the moment everyone waits for, and it lasts seconds.

- **Rehearse it** with the agency: when the music starts, when the cover lifts, when the lights change.
- **Two positions**: a wide shot of the stage and audience, and a closer shot of the car and presenters.
- **Burst shooting** through the moment the cover comes off.
- **The reaction**: guests and media photographing the car.
- **Leaders with the car** immediately after, before guests crowd it.

## Test drives and media drives

- **Cars on the road**: a planned route with safe photo points
- **Car-to-car shots** only with a professional driver and the right safety arrangements
- **Journalists driving and at stops**
- **Safety first**: photographers never stand on the road or in the path of vehicles.

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a car launch?
Hero shots of the car before guests arrive, the reveal, the brand's leadership and partners with the car, media and guests exploring it, details such as wheels and interior, and any test drives.

### How do you avoid reflections when photographing cars at events?
Use soft, large light sources, move or remove reflected people and objects, use a polariser for glass and paint, wear dark clothing, and check every angle before guests arrive.

### How do you photograph a car reveal?
Rehearse the timing with the agency, use two positions for a wide and a close shot, shoot in bursts as the cover comes off, then photograph guests' reactions and leaders with the car.

### Can photos be taken during test drives?
Yes, from planned safe photo points on the route. Car-to-car shots need a professional driver and proper safety arrangements, and photographers never stand on the road.

### How long does car launch photography take?
Usually a half day: hero shots before doors open, then the event. Media drives and multi-day previews are priced separately.
