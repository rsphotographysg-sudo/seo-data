# How Much Does Corporate Event Photography Cost in Singapore? (2026 Price Guide)

**Short answer:** In 2026, corporate event photography in Singapore typically costs **S$600–S$1,200 for a half day** and **S$1,500–S$2,800 for a full day** with one photographer. Conferences, gala dinners and multi-day MICE events usually run **S$2,000–S$3,500 or more**, depending on hours, number of photographers and how fast you need the photos.

Those figures are RS Photography's own nett rates, quoted with no GST added. The rest of this guide explains what moves a quote up or down, what should be included, and how to brief a photographer so the number you get is the number you pay.

### How this compares with freelance and marketplace rates

Marketplace listings in Singapore often quote **S$80–S$300 per hour** [EXTERNAL LINK: Airtasker Singapore event photographer price guide]. Those prices usually cover the photographer's time only. Editing, a backup photographer, insurance, a safety certification for government venues and guaranteed turnaround are often extra or not offered.

An agency quote is higher per hour because it prices the whole job: coverage, editing, delivery, backup equipment and someone accountable if a photographer falls sick on the day.

## What affects the cost of event photography

Five factors account for almost every difference between two quotes.

### 1. Hours of coverage

Hours are the biggest driver. Most quotes are built in half-day (up to 4 hours) and full-day (up to 8–9 hours) blocks. Ask whether the clock starts at guest arrival or at photographer call time, since setup and venue walk-throughs add 30–60 minutes.

### 2. Number of photographers

One photographer can cover a single-room seminar. You need two or more when:

- the stage and the networking area run at the same time
- there are breakout rooms or an exhibition floor
- the guest of honour arrives while the main programme is running
- you need a dedicated photographer for VIP portraits or a step-and-repeat backdrop

### 3. Turnaround time

Standard delivery is a preview set within 24–48 hours and the full edited gallery within 3–5 working days. **Same-day selects**, for press releases, LinkedIn posts or a closing-session slideshow, need an on-site editor and cost more.

### 4. Add-ons

- Event videography or a highlight reel
- Photo booth with instant prints
- Live streaming or hybrid-event AV
- Drone shots (in Singapore these require a CAAS-licensed operator and venue approval)
- Printed photos or albums for VIPs

Booking photo and video from one provider is usually cheaper than two separate vendors. It also means one crew briefing and one point of contact on the day.

### 5. Location and timing

Evening and weekend events, venues outside the city centre and events outside Singapore (Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok and beyond) add travel time and sometimes accommodation.

## What a good quote should include

Before you compare prices, check that each quote covers the same things:

- **Exact coverage hours** and the number of photographers
- **Editing**: colour correction and culling for every delivered image
- **Delivery format and timing**: online gallery, download links, preview timing
- **Usage rights** for marketing, annual reports and social media
- **Backup**: spare camera bodies, and a replacement photographer if someone falls ill
- **Price basis**: whether GST is included or added on top

RS Photography is **not GST-registered**, so every price we quote is the final nett amount.

## Ways to keep photography costs down

- **Book the hours you need.** If the important part is the 2-hour programme, don't book a full day for the coffee breaks.
- **Bundle photo and video** with one provider.
- **Share the run-of-show early** so the photographer is in place for key moments instead of covering everything "just in case".
- **Book early for peak season.** September to December fills fastest in Singapore because of conferences, annual dinners and year-end events.
