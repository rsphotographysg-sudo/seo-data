# How Many Photos Do You Get From an Event Photographer?

**Short answer:** As a rough guide, event photographers deliver around **50 to 150 edited photos per hour** of coverage, per photographer. The number depends on the event: a dinner with table photos produces more, a seminar fewer.

Every delivered photo should be **selected and edited**: duplicates, blinks and blurry shots removed, then exposure, colour, cropping and straightening corrected. **Detailed retouching** (skin, removing objects) and **RAW files** are usually not included unless agreed. **Ask for the expected number, editing level and delivery format in the quotation.**

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph corporate, MICE and government events in Singapore.*

## How many photos, by event type

These are rough guides per photographer per hour. Your photographer's numbers may differ.

| Event | Typical number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual dinner with table photos** | Higher end | Every table photographed, plus stage and candid moments |
| **Conference** | Middle | Speakers, panels, audience, booths and networking |
| **Award ceremony** | Higher end | One or more photos per recipient |
| **Seminar or workshop** | Lower end | Smaller groups and fewer distinct moments |
| **Ceremony or launch** | Middle | Short programme with key moments and VIP photos |
| **Headshots** | One to three chosen photos per person | Each person picks their best shot |

**Quality over quantity.** A gallery of 300 strong photos is more useful than 1,000 photos with repeats. Agree an approximate number before the event if it matters for your reporting.

---

## What "editing" includes

Standard event editing usually means:

- **Selection (culling)**: removing duplicates, closed eyes, blur and unflattering moments
- **Exposure and colour**: consistent brightness and natural skin tones across the gallery
- **Cropping and straightening**: level horizons and clean framing
- **Basic clean-up**: small distractions such as a stray cable at the edge

### What is usually extra: retouching
- **Skin retouching** for portraits and headshots
- **Removing objects or people** from a photo
- **Changing backgrounds** or combining photos
- **Detailed work** for advertising, annual reports or covers

If you need retouching for specific photos, such as the CEO's portrait or a cover image, **list them** and agree the price in advance. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-headshots-singapore "corporate headshots"] for portrait retouching levels.

## Do you get RAW files?

**Usually not.** RAW files are unprocessed camera files. They are large, need special software, and don't look finished. Most photographers deliver **edited JPEG files** only.

If you need RAW files, for example for your own design team, **ask before booking** and agree it in writing. Some photographers charge extra or don't offer them.

---

## How the photos are delivered

- **Online gallery**: a private link where you can view, share and download photos.
- **Two sizes**: **high resolution** for print and reports, and **web size** for social media and email.
- **Folders**: by session, speaker, sponsor, VIP or table, so people can find their photos quickly.
- **How long the gallery stays online**: ask, and **download and back up** the photos you need. Galleries are not permanent storage.
- **Same-day selects**: a small set during or right after the event. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "same-day event photo delivery"].

## When to expect the photos

A common standard in Singapore is a **preview set within 24–48 hours** and the **full gallery within 3–5 working days**. Large events or extra retouching take longer. Agree dates in the quotation.

## FAQ

### How many photos does an event photographer deliver per hour?
As a rough guide, around 50 to 150 edited photos per photographer per hour. Dinners with table photos and award ceremonies are usually at the higher end, and seminars at the lower end.

### What does event photo editing include?
Usually selection to remove duplicates, blinks and blur, plus exposure and colour correction, cropping, straightening and basic clean-up. Detailed retouching such as skin smoothing or removing objects is usually extra.

### Do event photographers give you RAW files?
Usually not. Most deliver edited JPEG files. If you need RAW files, ask before booking and agree it in writing, as some photographers charge extra or don't offer them.

### How are event photos delivered?
Usually through a private online gallery, with high-resolution and web-size files, organised in folders by session, speaker, sponsor or VIP. Download and back up the photos you need, because galleries are not permanent storage.

### Why don't we get every photo the photographer took?
Because photographers remove duplicates, closed eyes, blurry and unflattering shots. You receive the best version of each moment, already edited.
