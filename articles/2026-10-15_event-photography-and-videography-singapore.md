# Event Photography and Videography in Singapore: One Crew or Two Vendors?

**Short answer:** For most corporate events in Singapore, booking photography and videography from **one crew** is simpler and usually cheaper. You get one quote, one briefing and one point of contact on the day, and the photographer and videographer don't compete for the same spot.

Two separate vendors make sense when you need a specialist, for example a **full broadcast or live-stream production** or a **creative brand film** with its own director.

Whichever you choose, decide early **what video you need**, because that sets the crew, the audio setup and the cost.

## First decide what video you actually need

"Event video" can mean very different things. Pick the outputs before you ask for quotes:

| Video output | What it is | Typical length | Main use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Highlight reel** | Best moments edited to music | 60–120 seconds | Social media, website, next year's promotion |
| **Social cutdowns** | Short vertical clips | 15–30 seconds | LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok |
| **Session recordings** | Full keynotes or panels with clear audio | Length of each session | On-demand content, internal training, members' portal |
| **Speaker or testimonial clips** | Short interviews with speakers, delegates or sponsors | 30–90 seconds each | Marketing, sponsor reports |
| **Live stream** | Real-time broadcast to online viewers | Length of the programme | Hybrid events, remote audiences |

A highlight reel needs one videographer moving around the event. Session recordings need a fixed camera and a clean audio feed. A live stream needs a production setup. These are very different crews and budgets.

## One crew or two vendors?

| | One crew (photo + video) | Two separate vendors |
|---|---|---|
| **Briefing** | One brief, one run-of-show | Two briefs, and you coordinate between them |
| **On the day** | Crew plan positions together and don't block each other | Photographer and videographer can compete for the same spot |
| **Consistency** | Same style and colour across photos and video | Styles may differ |
| **Cost** | Usually lower, through shared setup and planning | Two minimum fees |
| **Accountability** | One vendor responsible for everything | Problems can fall between vendors |
| **Best when** | Most corporate events, conferences, dinners, launches | You need a specialist broadcast team or a brand-film director |

## Audio: the part most event videos get wrong

Viewers forgive average video, but not bad sound. Plan audio early:

- **Take a feed from the AV desk** for speeches and panels. It is the cleanest source in a ballroom or hall. Ask the venue's AV team in advance for a spare output.
- **Use lapel microphones** for interviews and testimonials.
- **Record backup audio** on a separate recorder in case the feed drops.
- **Interview in a quiet corner,** not next to the coffee station or the speaker stack.

---

## Music and permissions

- **Use licensed music** for highlight reels. Popular songs can get videos muted or removed on social platforms.
- **Get consent for interviews.** Ask each person filmed for an interview to agree to how the clip will be used.
- **Follow the same PDPA steps as for photography**: notices, an opt-out, and careful editing. See our [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/pdpa-event-photography-consent-guide "PDPA event photography consent guide"].
- **Check speaker permissions** before publishing full session recordings. Some speakers don't allow their slides or talk to be shared.

## Delivery times

- **Same-day or next-day highlight reels** are possible with an on-site editor. They are useful for closing-session screenings and social posts while the event is fresh.
- **Standard highlight reels**: usually delivered within a week or two, including one round of changes.
- **Session recordings**: depend on the number of sessions and whether slides are added into the video.
- **Photos alongside video**: preview within 24–48 hours, full gallery within a few working days. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "how same-day delivery works"].

Agree on the **number of revision rounds** in the quote. Unlimited changes are the most common cause of delayed and over-budget videos.

## How to brief a photo and video crew

Send one brief that covers both:

1. **Run-of-show** with times and rooms.
2. **Must-have moments**, marked as photo, video or both.
3. **Video outputs and lengths**, for example a 90-second highlight reel plus three 20-second vertical clips.
4. **Interview list**: who, when and what questions.
5. **AV contact** for the audio feed.
6. **Brand guidelines**: logo files, fonts and colours for titles and end cards.
7. **Music style**, for example upbeat, corporate or calm.
8. **Approver**: one person who reviews and signs off the edit.

## Budget

Video adds more to the budget than people expect, mainly because of editing time. As a guide:

- **Event photography**: see [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/corporate-event-photography-cost-singapore "corporate event photography costs in Singapore"]
- **Event videography**: from **S$2,000** per event for a highlight reel. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/event-videography-rates-singapore "event videography rates in Singapore"].
- **Photo + video from one crew**: usually less than booking the two separately, because planning, briefing and travel are shared.

What changes the video price most:

- number of camera operators
- number and length of edited videos
- interviews
- same-day editing
- live streaming
- revision rounds

## FAQ

### Should we book photography and videography from the same company?
For most corporate events, yes. One crew means one brief and one point of contact, and photographers and videographers can plan positions together. It is usually cheaper than two vendors. Use separate vendors when you need a specialist broadcast or brand-film team.

### What video do we need for a corporate event?
Most events need a 60 to 120 second highlight reel and a few short vertical clips for social media. Add session recordings if talks will be shared on demand, interview clips for sponsors or testimonials, and a live stream for hybrid events.

### How do we get good audio in an event video?
Take a feed from the venue's AV desk for speeches and panels, use lapel microphones for interviews, record backup audio separately, and film interviews in a quiet area.

### How long does an event highlight video take to deliver?
Same-day or next-day reels are possible with an on-site editor. Otherwise, highlight reels are usually delivered within one to two weeks, including one round of changes.

### How much does event videography cost in Singapore?
Event videography for a highlight reel typically starts from about S$2,000 per event. Costs rise with more camera operators, more edited videos, interviews, same-day editing and live streaming. Booking photo and video together usually costs less than booking them separately.
