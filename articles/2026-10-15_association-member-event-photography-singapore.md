# Association, AGM and Member Event Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Associations, chambers of commerce and member organisations run **many events a year**: AGMs, networking nights, seminars, orientations, awards and an annual dinner.

The photos have to keep **members engaged**, show **sponsors** they got value, and record the organisation's history.

The most efficient approach is usually **one photographer on a yearly arrangement** with:

- **fixed rates**
- a **consistent style**
- a **shared member gallery** for each event

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph AGMs, member networking events, seminars and annual dinners for associations and business chambers in Singapore.*

## The events associations usually run

| Event | What matters most |
|---|---|
| **AGM** | Council or board on stage, voting moments, outgoing and incoming office-bearers |
| **Networking nights** | Members meeting each other; many small groups; sponsors |
| **Seminars and talks** | Speakers, panels and an engaged audience |
| **New member orientations** | Welcome, introductions, group photo of the new intake |
| **Industry awards** | Every winner named; sponsors with winners |
| **Annual dinner or gala** | Guest of honour, sponsors, tables and entertainment |
| **Overseas missions and delegations** | Business visits, signings and meetings |

## AGM photography

AGMs are formal but short, and the photos become part of the organisation's record.

- **Council or board** at the head table or on stage
- **The president's address** and key presentations
- **Voting or show of hands**, if allowed to be photographed
- **Outgoing and incoming office-bearers**: handover moments and a group photo of the new council
- **Long-service or honorary awards**, if presented

Plan a **council group photo** right after the meeting, before people leave. Ask the secretariat for the final list of council members and their positions for captions.

---

## Networking events: photograph the members

At networking events, members want to see **themselves and their connections**, not just the stage.

- **Cover the whole room**: small groups, conversations and handshakes, not just VIPs.
- **Photograph sponsors and partners** with members and the leadership.
- **Capture name tags** only where appropriate. Members may not want their name visible in public posts.
- **Deliver quickly.** Members share photos on LinkedIn the next day.

A **roaming photographer** works best. Add a **backdrop photo spot** with the association's and sponsors' logos for posed photos.

---

## Sponsors: show them the value

Sponsors fund many association events. Photos help renew those sponsorships:

- **Logos visible**: backdrops, banners, stage screens and tables
- **Sponsor representatives** with the president, guest of honour and members
- **Sponsor booths or tables** with members engaging
- **A sponsor folder** in each gallery, so the secretariat can send photos easily

## Member galleries and privacy

- **A private gallery per event**, shared with members by link or through the member portal.
- **Organised by segment**: arrivals, speeches, networking, awards and the group photo.
- **Opt-out**: tell attendees in the invitation and at registration that photography is taking place, and offer a way to opt out. See our [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/pdpa-event-photography-consent-guide "PDPA event photography consent guide"].
- **Public use**: for the association's website or social media, choose photos where members look good, and remove any on request.

## FAQ

### What should we photograph at an AGM?
The council or board on stage, the president's address, voting if allowed, outgoing and incoming office-bearers, any awards, and a group photo of the new council right after the meeting.

### How do we photograph a networking event well?
Use a roaming photographer to cover small groups, conversations and handshakes across the whole room, photograph sponsors with members and leaders, add a branded backdrop for posed photos, and deliver quickly so members can share the next day.

### How can photos help with sponsor renewals?
Make logos visible in photos, photograph sponsor representatives with leaders and members, cover sponsor booths or tables, and keep a sponsor folder in each gallery so photos can be sent to sponsors easily.

### Should an association use one photographer for all its events?
Usually yes. A yearly arrangement gives fixed rates, a consistent style, a photographer who knows the council and key members, priority booking, and easier budgeting.

### How should we share photos with members?
Use a private gallery for each event, organised by segment, shared by link or through the member portal. Tell attendees about photography in advance, offer an opt-out, and remove photos on request.
