# Corporate and Charity Golf Day Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Corporate and charity golf days are spread across a whole course for five or six hours, so plan photography around **fixed moments**:

- **a photo of every flight** at the first tee before they start
- **the flag-off** by the guest of honour
- **sponsor and contest holes**, such as hole-in-one and nearest-to-the-pin
- **action shots** taken from a buggy, **without disturbing play**
- **the prize presentation dinner**, with every winner and sponsor

Follow **golf etiquette** (no noise or movement during a swing) and the **club's rules**, and plan for **heat and lightning delays**.

## Before tee-off

- **Registration and goodie bags**: guests arriving and checking in
- **Sponsor backdrop**: guests photographed in front of the event backdrop
- **The briefing**: rules, format and safety
- **The flag-off**: the guest of honour starting the event, often with a horn or a ceremonial tee shot
- **Flight photos**: every group of three or four players at the first tee, or at their starting hole for a shotgun start

**Flight photos are the most requested photos of the day.** Plan time for them in the programme, especially for a shotgun start, where flights go to different holes at the same time.

---

## On the course

A photographer in a **buggy** moves around the course to photograph flights in play.

- **Golf etiquette**: no clicks, movement or talking during a swing. Photograph from a safe angle, out of the player's line of sight.
- **Safety**: stay well clear of the line of play and behind players, never in front.
- **Action**: drives, chips and putts, celebrations and reactions.
- **Each flight at least once** on the course, with a list to tick off.
- **Course scenery**: wide shots of the course, the clubhouse and the event signage.

---

## Sponsor holes and contests

- **Hole sponsors**: photograph each sponsor's signage and booth, ideally with players.
- **Contest holes**: hole-in-one, nearest-to-the-pin and longest drive, with the prize on display.
- **Refreshment stations** and on-course activities.
- **A sponsor folder** in the gallery for quick delivery after the event.

---

## Weather and lightning

Golf days in Singapore face heat and afternoon thunderstorms.

- **Lightning**: golf clubs in Singapore suspend play when there is lightning nearby. Photographers follow the same rules and take shelter.
- **Heat**: hats, water and shade for the crew; early tee times help.
- **Rain**: rain covers for cameras, and photos of the clubhouse during delays.

## Club rules

- **Ask the club** about rules for photography and video on the course, and any areas that are off-limits.
- **Dress code**: photographers usually follow the club's dress code, such as collared shirts and suitable shoes.
- **Buggy access**: arrange a buggy for the photographer with the club or organiser.
- **Drones**: usually need the club's permission, a licensed operator and the right permits.

## FAQ

### What should be photographed at a corporate golf day?
Registration and the sponsor backdrop, the briefing and flag-off, a photo of every flight at the first tee or starting hole, players in action on the course, sponsor and contest holes, and the prize presentation dinner with every winner and sponsor.

### How do you photograph golfers without disturbing play?
Stay out of the player's line of sight and well behind the line of play, make no noise or movement during the swing, and shoot from a safe angle. Photographers follow golf etiquette and the club's rules.

### How are flight photos taken at a shotgun start?
Plan them before the start, at the first tee or as flights leave for their starting holes, or have photographers at several holes. Flight photos are the most requested photos of the day.

### What happens to golf day photography when there is lightning?
Golf clubs in Singapore suspend play when there is lightning nearby, and photographers take shelter too. Use delays to photograph the clubhouse and guests, and continue when play resumes.

### How many photographers does a golf day need?
Usually one for the course and dinner, or two for a large field or a shotgun start, so one can cover the start and course while the other covers sponsor holes and the dinner.
