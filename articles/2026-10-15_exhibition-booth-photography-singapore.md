# Exhibition and Trade Show Booth Photography in Singapore

**Short answer:** Good exhibition photography is about timing.

- Shoot the **clean, empty booth** before doors open.
- Shoot the **busy booth** at peak visitor hours.
- Be at the booth for **VIP visits** and demos, which usually happen at set times.

Exhibitors usually need a short booth session of 1–2 hours per day. Organisers need full-day coverage of the opening, the show floor and any conference stage. Crew need exhibitor or contractor passes, so arrange them before move-in.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph exhibitions, trade shows and MICE events at Singapore's major venues.*

## Exhibitor or organiser: two different jobs

| | Exhibitor | Organiser |
|---|---|---|
| **Main goal** | Marketing material and proof of results for one booth | Coverage of the whole show for marketing, sponsors and next year's sales |
| **Typical coverage** | 1–2 hours per day, timed to peak moments | Full day, often across several days |
| **Must-have shots** | Booth, team, visitors, demos, VIP visits | Opening ceremony, guest of honour tour, busy aisles, conference stage, sponsor booths |
| **Who uses the photos** | Marketing, sales and regional head office | Marketing, sponsorship sales, media and partners |

Many organisers also offer exhibitors a **photography package**: one photographer visits each participating booth at an agreed time. It is often cheaper for exhibitors than booking their own photographer, and it gives the organiser more show photos.

## Exhibitor shot list

### Before doors open
- [ ] **The empty booth**, clean, lights on, screens running, from the aisle and from each corner
- [ ] Product displays and signage, close up
- [ ] **The booth team**, together in front of the booth

### During the show
- [ ] **The busy booth** at peak hours, with visitors engaging with staff
- [ ] Live demos and presentations, with the audience in frame
- [ ] One-on-one conversations, natural and not posed
- [ ] Lucky draws, giveaways or activities at the booth

### Special moments
- [ ] **VIP or guest of honour visit**: arrival at the booth, handshake, product explanation, group photo
- [ ] Signing ceremonies (MOUs or partnership agreements)
- [ ] Media interviews at the booth

### For your post-show report
- [ ] Wide shot showing the booth in the context of the hall
- [ ] Visitor queue or crowd shots
- [ ] Your logo visible in every key photo

## When to shoot: timing makes the photos

- **Empty-booth photos: the first morning, 30–60 minutes before doors open.** By the afternoon, booths are worn, cluttered and full of bags and boxes.
- **Busy-booth photos: peak hours.** Usually late morning and just after lunch on the first one or two days. Trade-only days are often busier with buyers; public days with families.
- **VIP tour: follow the official route.** Get the route and timing from the organiser. VIPs often spend only a minute or two at each booth, so the photographer must be in place before they arrive.
- **Demos: at scheduled times.** Put demo times in the brief, so the photographer is there when the audience is largest.

## Logistics at Singapore venues

Exhibitions in Singapore are mostly held at **Marina Bay Sands Expo and Convention Centre**, **Singapore EXPO** and **Suntec Singapore**. They are large venues with their own rules. Wherever your show is:

- **Passes.** Crew usually need exhibitor badges or contractor passes. Ask your organiser how to apply, and send crew names early.
- **Move-in and opening hours.** For empty-booth photos before doors open, crew must be allowed in early. Confirm with the organiser.
- **Loading and parking.** Equipment comes through the loading bay or a specific entrance. Check the rules for your hall.
- **Distances.** Halls are large. Walking from one end to the other can take several minutes, which matters when following a VIP tour or covering several booths.
- **Lighting.** Hall lighting is mixed and booths often have their own spotlights and LED screens. A photographer with flash and experience of exhibition halls will get even, accurate colour.

## Booth photography tips for exhibitors

- **Clean the booth before the photographer arrives.** Hide bags, boxes, cables and drinks. Switch on screens and lighting.
- **Dress the team in booth uniform** for the team photo.
- **Tell your staff who the photographer is,** so they engage naturally with visitors instead of looking at the camera.
- **Plan the VIP moment.** Decide who greets the VIP, what product is shown and where the group photo is taken.
- **Ask for fast delivery** if you want to post on LinkedIn during the show. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/same-day-event-photo-delivery-singapore "how same-day delivery works"].

## FAQ

### When is the best time to photograph an exhibition booth?
Photograph the clean, empty booth 30 to 60 minutes before doors open on the first morning. Photograph the busy booth at peak hours, usually late morning and after lunch on the first one or two days.

### How long does an exhibitor booth photography session take?
Usually one to two hours per day, timed to cover the empty booth, the busy booth at peak hours, demos and any VIP visit.

### Do photographers need passes for exhibitions in Singapore?
Usually yes. Crew normally need exhibitor badges or contractor passes from the organiser. Apply early and confirm whether early access before opening is allowed.

### Should organisers offer photography packages to exhibitors?
Often yes. One photographer visiting participating booths at agreed times is usually cheaper for exhibitors than booking their own, and it gives the organiser more show photos.

### What should exhibitors prepare before the photographer arrives?
Clean the booth, hide bags and boxes, switch on screens and lighting, dress the team in booth uniform, and share demo times and any VIP visit schedule.
