# School Event Photography in Singapore: A Planning Guide

**Short answer:** School events need the same things as corporate events, plus three more:

- **consent arrangements for students**
- **photographers comfortable working with children and young people**
- **delivery that suits school uses**: the school magazine, website, social media and records

For a speech day or anniversary celebration, plan one photographer for the hall programme and one for the guest of honour, performances and visitors if the school is large. Book early for **anniversary years** and **end-of-year ceremonies**, which fill up fast.

| Event | What matters most |
|---|---|

| **Principal, staff and student interviews** | Portraits and short videos for the website or anniversary content |

## Consent and child protection come first

Photographing students needs more care than photographing adults.

- **Follow the school's consent process.** Many schools already collect parental consent for photography at the start of the year. Confirm which students have **not** consented, and how they will be identified on the day.
- **Brief every photographer** on students who must not be photographed or published.
- **No personal use.** Photos belong to the school. Photographers should not post them on personal social media or portfolios without written permission.
- **Secure delivery.** Use a private gallery or school drive, not a public link.
- **Careful selection.** Leave out photos that show name tags, addresses or anything that identifies a student more than needed.
- **Crew conduct.** Photographers should stay in public areas of the school, never be alone with a student, and follow the school's visitor rules.

## School anniversary photography

An anniversary is the most important photography job a school has in a decade. The photos end up in commemorative books, exhibitions and videos, and are used for years.

Plan for:

- **The celebration itself**: guest of honour arrival, speeches, performances, unveilings and the cake-cutting or time-capsule moment.
- **Alumni and former principals**: group photos across generations.
- **The school today**: classrooms, labs, sports, CCAs and staff, photographed before the event for the commemorative book.
- **Interviews**: principal, long-serving staff, alumni and students on video, for an anniversary film.
- **Archive quality**: high-resolution files, properly named and stored, so they can be found again in ten years.

Start planning **six months ahead** if the anniversary includes a book or a video. Content for both has to be shot well before the celebration.

## Speech day and prize-giving

The main job is photographing **every prize winner on stage, correctly named**.

- Give the photographer the **prize list in order**, by category.
- Use **stage marks** for the presenter and student, and a short pause facing the camera.
- Plan **group photos** of each category's winners and of top award recipients with the guest of honour.
- Ask for **photos named by student**, so teachers can share them with families easily.

The method is the same as for other award ceremonies. See [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/award-ceremony-photography-singapore "award ceremony photography"] for stage blocking and naming in detail.

## Class and group photos

Class photos are a big job with tight timing. To run them smoothly:

- **Set up one location** with risers or benches, shade and a consistent background.
- **Schedule classes in slots**, for example every 5–8 minutes, coordinated with timetables.
- **Use a standard layout** (tallest at the back, teachers on the sides) so all photos match.
- **Have a teacher with each class** to manage students and check names.
- **Plan a rain backup** indoors.
- **Agree on name lists** for captions, if the photos go into the yearbook or magazine.

## Sports day and outdoor events

- **Heat and light.** Outdoor events in Singapore mean harsh midday sun and the chance of rain. Plan shade for the crew and a wet-weather plan.
- **Action shots** need longer lenses and positions near the finish line and key events. Agree where photographers may stand on the track or field.
- **House spirit** photos of cheering and banners are often the most used photos afterwards.

## FAQ

### How do schools handle consent for event photography?
Most schools collect parental consent at the start of the year. Before the event, confirm which students have not consented and how they will be identified, and brief every photographer. Photos should be delivered securely and not used by the photographer without written permission.

### How early should a school book a photographer for its anniversary?
Six months ahead if the anniversary includes a commemorative book or video, because content for both has to be shot before the celebration. For the celebration day alone, two to three months ahead.

### How do we make sure every prize winner is photographed at speech day?
Give the photographer the prize list in order, use stage marks and a short pause facing the camera, and ask for photos named by student. Plan group photos for each award category.

### How long do class photos take?
Plan about 5 to 8 minutes per class at one fixed location with risers, with a teacher accompanying each class. A typical school can complete class photos in one or two days, depending on the number of classes.

### Can the photographer also film interviews for an anniversary video?
Yes. Many schools combine anniversary photography with interviews of the principal, long-serving staff, alumni and students. Plan these shoots in the months before the celebration.
