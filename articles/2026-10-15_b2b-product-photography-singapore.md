# B2B Product Photography in Singapore: A Planning Guide

**Short answer:** B2B product photos need to be **accurate first** and attractive second. Buyers use them to check specifications, compare models and justify a purchase. Plan three kinds of image:

- **Catalogue shots**: consistent angles on a clean background, with accurate colour
- **Detail shots**: connectors, controls, labels and materials
- **In-use shots**: the product working in a real setting, with people

Agree a **shot list per product**, **file specifications** and **usage rights** before the shoot. Large equipment is usually photographed **on site** rather than in a studio.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. We photograph products, equipment and facilities for B2B companies in Singapore, alongside corporate and event photography.*

## Types of B2B product image

| Type | What it shows | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Catalogue / white background** | The whole product, consistent angles, true colour | Catalogues, websites, e-commerce, tenders and spec sheets |
| **Detail** | Ports, controls, labels, textures and finishes | Spec sheets, technical pages, sales presentations |
| **In use / application** | The product working, with people, in a realistic setting | Brochures, websites, trade shows, social media |
| **Group / range** | A product family together | Range pages, covers, trade show graphics |
| **Packaging** | Boxes, labels and contents | Distributors, e-commerce, retail listings |
| **Scale** | The product next to a person or a known object | Large equipment, where size isn't obvious |

## Planning the shoot

### 1. List products and variants
List every product, model and colour variant to be photographed. Note which are priorities and which can share a setup.

### 2. Set a standard shot list
For catalogue consistency, use the **same angles for every product**, for example:
- front
- 45° front-left
- side
- back
- top
- 2–3 details

### 3. Agree file specifications
- **Background**: pure white, transparent (cut-out) or a set colour
- **Aspect ratio and size**: square for e-commerce, wider for web banners
- **Margins**: the same space around each product, so a product grid lines up
- **File formats**: high-resolution files for print, web-optimised files, and cut-outs where needed
- **File naming**: model number and angle, for example `RX200_front.jpg`

### 4. Prepare the products
- clean, dust-free and fingerprint-free
- protective film removed
- cables tidied
- screens showing a clean interface
- labels straight

Damaged or prototype units should be flagged in advance.

## Colour accuracy

For B2B buyers, wrong colour can mean a returned order or a failed tender. To get colour right:

- **Use a colour reference card** in a test shot for each setup.
- **Shoot under controlled light** in a studio or a light tent.
- **Compare with the physical product** during the shoot.
- **Agree on brand colours** for products that must match corporate colours exactly.

---

## Large equipment and on-site shoots

Machinery, medical equipment, vehicles and installations are usually too large or too sensitive to move.

- **Shoot at the client's premises**, in a showroom, factory or installed site.
- **Clear the area** of clutter, cables, tools and signage you don't want shown.
- **Bring lighting** to control shadows and reflections on metal and glass.
- **Follow site rules**: PPE, safety briefings and restricted areas in factories, cleanrooms, hospitals and construction sites. RS Photography is bizSAFE Level 3 certified.
- **Show scale** with a person operating or standing near the equipment.

## In-use and lifestyle images

In-use images help buyers picture the product in their own operations.

- **Real settings**: a working lab, office, factory floor, clinic or site.
- **Real roles**: staff or models dressed for the job, with the correct PPE.
- **Show the benefit**: the product solving a problem, not just sitting there.
- **Releases**: anyone identifiable signs a model release, and site owners give permission.

---

## Retouching

- **Standard**: dust and scratch removal, colour correction, background cleaning and consistent margins.
- **Cut-outs**: clean edges for transparent backgrounds.
- **Composites**: combining shots, for example a screen image inserted or reflections removed. Keep these honest. B2B buyers expect the photo to match what they receive.

---

## Usage rights

Confirm the photos can be used:
- by your company and, if needed, by **distributors and resellers**
- in **print, web, e-commerce marketplaces and advertising**
- in **tender submissions and trade show graphics**
- without a time limit, if needed

## FAQ

### What product photos does a B2B company need?
Usually catalogue shots on a clean background with consistent angles, detail shots of controls and connectors, in-use shots in real settings, and sometimes range, packaging and scale shots for large equipment.

### How do we keep product photos consistent across a catalogue?
Use the same angles, background, lighting, margins and aspect ratio for every product, and name files by model number and angle. Record the setup so new products can be added later to match.

### How do we make sure product colours are accurate?
Shoot under controlled light with a colour reference card, compare the images with the physical product during the shoot, and agree on exact brand colours in advance.

### Can large machinery or medical equipment be photographed?
Yes. Large or sensitive equipment is usually photographed on site, in a showroom, factory or installation, with portable lighting. The area is cleared first, and the crew follows site safety and access rules.

### Can distributors use our product photos?
Only if the usage rights allow it. Agree in the quotation whether distributors and resellers may use the photos, and in which channels, such as e-commerce marketplaces, print and advertising.
