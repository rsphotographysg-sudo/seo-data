# Corporate Event Photography in Singapore: The Complete Guide

**Short answer:** Good corporate event photography in Singapore comes down to four decisions:

- **budget**: typically S$600–S$1,200 for a half day and S$1,500–S$2,800 for a full day with one photographer
- **the right vendor**: full galleries, backup, compliance and a clear nett quote
- **preparation**: run-of-show, shot list, speaker list and venue details sent a week before
- **delivery**: preview in 24–48 hours, or same-day selects when the photos are needed on the day

This guide brings together everything we have written on the topic, with links to a detailed guide for each part.

*Written by Ricky Ee, Director of RS Photography. RS Photography has photographed corporate, MICE and government events in Singapore since 2013, with an in-house team covering photography, video, live streaming and photo booths.*

---

## 1. Budget and pricing

In Singapore, corporate event photography with one photographer typically costs:

- **S$600–S$1,200** for a half day
- **S$1,500–S$2,800** for a full day
- **S$2,000–S$3,500 or more** for gala dinners and MICE events

The price depends mostly on hours, number of photographers and how fast you need the photos.

## 2. Choosing a photographer

Ask to see **full galleries** from events like yours, not just highlights. Confirm what happens if the photographer falls sick, get delivery times and usage rights in writing, and check the certifications your organisation needs.

→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/how-to-choose-corporate-event-photographer-singapore "How to choose a corporate event photographer: 10 questions to ask"]

## 3. Planning and preparation

Send your photographer four things at least a week before:

- the run-of-show
- a shot list
- the speaker and VIP list with headshots
- venue details

Plan the group photo and make sure speakers' faces are lit.

→ [INTERNAL LINK: /insights/conference-photography-checklist "Conference photography checklist for organisers"]

## 4. Delivery and turnaround

Standard delivery is a **preview set within 24–48 hours** and the **full gallery within 3–5 working days**. With an on-site editor, first selects can arrive **within the hour** for press releases and social media.

## 8. Privacy and consent

Photos of identifiable people are personal data. For every event:

- tell guests that photography is taking place
- offer an opt-out, such as a coloured lanyard
- brief every photographer on it
- deliver photos through a private gallery

Take extra care with children, patients and vulnerable people.

## FAQ

### How much does corporate event photography cost in Singapore?
Typically S$600 to S$1,200 for a half day and S$1,500 to S$2,800 for a full day with one photographer. Gala dinners and MICE events usually run S$2,000 to S$3,500 or more, depending on hours, photographers and turnaround.

### How do I choose a corporate event photographer in Singapore?
Ask for full galleries from events like yours, confirm backup photographers and equipment, get delivery times and usage rights in writing, check the certifications your organisation needs, and compare itemised nett quotes.

### How fast are event photos delivered?
Usually a preview set within 24 to 48 hours and the full edited gallery within 3 to 5 working days. With an on-site editor, first selects can be delivered within the hour.

### What should we prepare before the event?
The run-of-show, a shot list of must-have moments, the speaker and VIP list with headshots, venue details and one day-of contact, sent at least a week before the event.

### Can one company provide photography, video, live streaming and photo booths?
Yes. Booking these from one team means one brief, one point of contact and consistent style, and it usually costs less than booking separate vendors.
