## Lighting Conditions by Venue

### Marina Bay Sands Expo
- **Main halls:** Fluorescent + LED mixed. Natural light through skylights. Flash recommended for stage/keynote shots.
- **Ballroom:** Professional theatrical lighting (DMX-controlled). Excellent for awards ceremonies.
- **Foyers:** Good natural light during daytime events.
- **Photography tip:** Bring flash for stage work. White balance set to 5600K for ballroom, 6500K for foyer.

### Suntec Singapore Convention
- **Halls 1-6:** Good overhead fluorescent. Less natural light.
- **Convention Level 4:** Excellent natural light through glass façade. Great for networking shots.
- **Ballroom:** Warm ambient lighting. External flash recommended.
- **Photography tip:** Wide-angle lens essential for hall coverage (17-35mm recommended).

### Resorts World Sentosa
- **Convention Centre:** Variable. Ballroom is dark and atmospheric — fast lens needed (f/2.8 or wider).
- **Forum:** Good natural light.
- **Outdoor zones:** Tropical sunlight. Polarising filter recommended.
- **Photography tip:** f/2.8 minimum for ballroom work. Watch for mixed tungsten/fluorescent in ballrooms.

### Singapore EXPO
- **Halls 1-7:** Excellent high-bay LED lighting. Some of the best-lit exhibition halls in SEA.
- **ECTC Hall:** Wide-span column-free. Wide-angle lens essential.
- **Photography tip:** Natural light supplement through roof skylights. No flash needed in main halls.

## Logistics & Accessibility

### Marina Bay Sands
- **Vendor registration:** Required 3 business days before event. Submit via MBS vendor portal.
- **Access points:** Loading bay at Basement 1. Photographer badge from organiser required.
- **Equipment restrictions:** Tripods permitted in public areas. Stage photography without flash during performances — check with organiser.
- **Carrying:** Large venue — allow 10 min between halls.
- **Parking:** MBS underground — S$3.20/hr, max S$25/day.
- **Catering:** Catering by hotel only. No outside F&B.

### Suntec Singapore
- **Vendor registration:** Submit vendor form to events team. 2 business days minimum.
- **Access points:** Loading bay Level 1. Photographer entry via main entrance with badge.
- **Equipment restrictions:** Standard — no special restrictions.
- **Carrying:** Connected to Suntec City mall. Allow transfer time between halls.
- **Parking:** Suntec City Mall — S$2.50/hr.

### Resorts World Sentosa
- **Vendor registration:** Advance permit required. Allow 5 business days.
- **Access points:** Sentosa Gateway entrance. Vehicle pass required for loading.
- **Equipment restrictions:** Special events may restrict large bags.
- **Carrying:** Resort territory — electric buggy transport available (arrange with organiser).
- **Parking:** RWS carparks — S$4/hr. Free for hotel guests.

### Singapore EXPO
- **Vendor registration:** Venue access form required. 3 business days minimum.
- **Access points:** Main entrance with organiser badge. Loading dock at rear.
- **Equipment restrictions:** Standard — wide-aisle venue.
- **Carrying:** Massive venue — 7 halls. Buggy transport available.
- **Parking:** On-site — S$2.40/hr.

## Government Compliance Notes

For events involving Singapore government agencies (WOG):

- **Photographer identification:** Valid event pass required at all times. Display prominently.
- **Image approval:** All published images may require agency approval before commercial use.
- **File retention:** Some agencies require 12-month file retention post-event.
- **Security clearance:** Some venues require advance security clearance for photographers.
- **No GST:** RS Photography is not GST-registered. All-in pricing for government events.
