# Growth plan — the ladder to a very large RS

Ricky's stated ambition (30 Sep 2026): **S$1 billion in yearly sales within 2–3 years,
with the business fully automated.** Actual revenue, pipeline and targets in dollars are
kept in the Notion Master Index; this page uses public-safe maths only.

## The honest maths first

Photography and video is a service business. Revenue = jobs × average job value.

| To reach S$1B a year at… | You would need… |
|---|---|
| ~S$3,000 average job | ~333,000 jobs a year ≈ **900 jobs every day** |
| ~S$15,000 average job (tender-size) | ~67,000 jobs a year ≈ 180 a day |
| S$500/month software subscription | ~167,000 paying customers |

S$1B is several hundred times RS's current revenue. No photography studio in Singapore,
or anywhere in the region, operates at that scale, and the Singapore corporate and MICE
photography market is far smaller than S$1B in total. Services alone cannot get there
in 2–3 years, however well automated.

So the plan keeps the ambition and changes the vehicle: **grow the service business
hard with automation, and use the cash and data it produces to build products that are
not limited by crew hours.** Each stage has a gate; we only move up when the gate is met.

## Stage 1 — make RS a machine (now → end 2027): target 2–3× today's revenue

The bottleneck today is not leads. It is **quotes and tenders waiting on Ricky** and
**tender prices set well above the winning bids**. Fix those first.

- One-tap daily approval list for every draft (automation.md → A1).
- Enquiry → quote draft in under 15 minutes, off the locked rate card (A2, A3).
- Tender engine: find, read, price against past winning bids, draft (A4).
- Crew auto-scheduling from skills and geography; subcontractor bench for peaks (A6).
- Review ask and repeat-client nudges on every delivery (A9).
- Incorporate (Pte Ltd) and public-liability insurance so bigger tenders are open to us (bizSAFE Level 3 held since Sep 2026).

**Gate:** 50+ qualified enquiries a month, quote turnaround < 1 working day,
tender win rate doubled, Ricky's daily admin < 1 hour.

## Stage 2 — region and add-ons (2028): target ~10× today's revenue

- Regional partner-photographer network (KL, Jakarta, Bangkok, Sydney, Melbourne) —
  RS sells, schedules, QCs and invoices; partners shoot. Software is what makes this
  possible without hiring coordinators.
- LIVE 60™ sold as a paid add-on on RS jobs (photos delivered live during the event).
- Retainers with agencies, venues and government panels instead of one-off jobs.

**Gate:** partners deliver to RS standard with < 2% complaints; LIVE 60™ attached to
≥ 20% of MICE jobs.

## Stage 3 — products and capital (2029+): where a very large number is possible

- **LIVE 60™ as SaaS** for other photographers and event organisers worldwide — revenue
  not limited by RS's crew.
- **Roll-up:** buy smaller studios and run them on the RS automation stack (needs the
  Pte Ltd, audited accounts and outside capital).
- Event-media marketplace for the region, if Stage 2 proves the partner model.

**Gate:** product revenue growing without adding crew; investors or lenders lined up.

## Scoreboard (tracked weekly in the Notion Master Index)

- Qualified enquiries / month
- Quote turnaround (hours)
- Drafts waiting on Ricky, and their age
- Tender win rate and price gap to winner
- Average job value
- Revenue per crew member
- % of jobs that needed zero Ricky time between booking and payment
- LIVE 60™ attach rate (from Stage 2)
