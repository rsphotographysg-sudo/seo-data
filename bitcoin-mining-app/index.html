Part 4 · Scam shield

If you ever see these, walk away

  • “Generate / flash / double your BTC” apps or videos — bitcoin cannot be generated by software; the protocol makes it mathematically impossible.
  • Cloud-mining sites showing a balance that grows fast but demands a “fee”, “tax” or “upgrade” before you can withdraw.
  • Anyone — including a “support agent” or a new online friend — asking for your seed phrase or private key.
  • Guaranteed daily returns (“1% a day”, “1 BTC in 24 hours”). Guaranteed + crypto = Ponzi.
  • Pressure to move the chat to Telegram/WhatsApp and send funds to “activate a mining node”.
